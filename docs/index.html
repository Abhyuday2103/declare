      How to play

      Everyone gets 4 cards face down in a 2 by 2 grid. You see your bottom two once at the start. Then it's memory.

      Goal. Empty your grid, or hold the lowest total. A is 1, J 11, Q 12, red K 13, black K 0.

      Your turn. Draw from the deck. Swap it into any of your cards, or throw it straight on the pile. What lands on the pile from your turn uses its power.

      • 7, 8 look at one of your own cards.
      • 9, 10 look at one card of someone else.
      • J skip the next player.
      • Q swap any two cards between two players, blind.
      • K look at one of yours and one of theirs, then swap any two if you want.

      Snap. When a card hits the pile, anyone can throw a card of the same rank on it until the next player draws. Snap your own to get rid of it. Snap someone else's and you hand them one of yours into the gap. Wrong card means a penalty card face down. The app never tells you a snap is possible.

      Declare. At the start of your turn with 2 cards or fewer, call your exact total. Right, you win. Wrong, you take a penalty card.
